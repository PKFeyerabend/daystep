Daystep privacy policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Daystep is a habit tracker made by Feyerabend Studios ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Daystep app for Android.

The short version: your habits, notes and photos stay on your phone. Daystep has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. We don't collect, see or sell any of your data.

What Daystep stores, and where

Everything you enter is stored only on your device, in Daystep's private app storage:

  • the habits you create and their settings (names, schedules, reminder times)
  • what you log each day, including any notes
  • photos you attach to a log. Daystep saves a smaller copy of the photo you pick. The original stays where it was.
  • your app settings, such as theme and notification preferences

Daystep doesn't send any of this to us or to anyone else. We have no servers that receive it.

Backups

If Android's own backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include Daystep's data in your device backup. That backup is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy, not by us. Daystep leaves photos out of cloud backups. They are included when you move to a new phone with Android's phone-to-phone transfer.

You can also save a backup file yourself from Settings › Your data. It contains your habits, logs, notes, photos and settings, and Daystep writes it only to the place you pick, such as Google Drive or your phone's storage. Once saved, the file is handled by wherever you chose to keep it. We never receive a copy.

Purchases

Daystep Pro is a one-off purchase made through Google Play. Google processes the payment and holds your payment details. Daystep only asks Google Play whether you own Pro, so it can unlock unlimited habits, including after a reinstall. See Google's privacy policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Daystep schedules reminders and encouragement messages on your phone. They are created on the device from your own habit data, and nothing is sent over the internet to produce them.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the reminders and encouragement you've turned on.
  • Billing (Google Play): to offer and check the Pro purchase. Google's billing library also needs internet access to reach Google Play.
  • Run in the background and after restart: so scheduled reminders still arrive after your phone restarts.

Daystep picks photos through Android's photo picker, so it never gets access to your whole photo library.

Deleting your data

  • To delete one habit and its history, notes and photos, open the habit and tap the bin icon.
  • To delete everything, uninstall Daystep or clear its storage in your phone's settings. When you uninstall, Android may offer to keep Daystep's data for a reinstall; say no to delete it.
  • Backup files you've saved aren't affected. Delete them from wherever you saved them.

Because we never receive your data, there's nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Daystep isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the effective date at the top. If a change affects how your data is handled, we'll also say so in the app's release notes.
